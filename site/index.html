Nightwater

Every answer is a slide.
From addition to calculus.

A first-person math game in a water park after dark. Each pool asks a question, and its three exit pipes are the answers.

Play in your browser

Plays in the browser on desktop and phone. Headphones recommended.

Try it: answer to slide down

Each pool asks a question. Choose an exit to ride it down.

Lean into the curves.

Every exit is a flume. Choose one and you ride it down to the next pool.

Lean into the tube wall with A and D or the arrow keys, or with your left thumb on a phone. Your best catch multiplies the points for your next answer.

Catch the lights.

Multiply your score.

100 points times the level number times your multiplier: level 10 with a star is worth 10,000 points. 100points × 10level × 10star = 10,000points

Answer correctly for 100 points × the level number × your multiplier, plus up to half as much again for answering quickly.

Answer wrong and it costs 75% of that stake, never taking your score below zero, and brings a fresh question at the same level.

21 stages · 210 levels

From addition to calculus.

Each stage has 10 levels. Every level makes fresh questions from its own rules, each with three choices and one correct answer.

You start at Stage 1 and move up a level with every correct answer. Progress saves in your browser.

    What's on screen
    1. A slow pan across the first pool at night to three glowing exits. The park is yours. After hours, forever.
    2. The question 7 × 7 = ? appears on the wall. Every answer is a slide.
    3. The rider takes the mint exit into its flume. Lean into the curves.
    4. Lights rush past and are caught, then the star. Catch the lights. Multiply your score.
    5. The flume launches the rider over the next pool: 42 ÷ 7 = ?, then the gold exit.
    6. A montage of questions from fractions, geometry and calculus. 21 stages · 210 levels. From addition to calculus.
    7. Nightwater. Every answer is a slide. Play in your browser: math.oddessentials.ai.

    Headphones recommended.

    Water rushes under you, every light you catch rings a note, and each pool greets you with a splash.

    An 18-track album plays as you ride, louder in the flumes and softer in the pools.

    Streams from the game's own server when you press play.

    Plays in your browser.

    On a computer with keyboard and mouse, or on a phone with your thumbs. There is nothing to install.

    Controls
    Action Keyboard and mouse Touch
    Lean in the tube A / D or ← / → Drag the thumb pad
    Paddle in a pool W A S D Drag the thumb pad
    Look around Drag, or click to capture the mouse Drag
    Choose an exit 1 / 2 / 3, or click an answer Tap an answer
    Pause and sound Esc and M On-screen buttons
    • The pause menu sets the ride speed (Relaxed, Fast or Rush) and the rendering quality.
    • Progress saves in your browser, so there's no account to make.
    • A wrong answer never ends the run. You get a fresh question at the same level.
    • Clear all 210 levels and you can keep riding. The descent never ends.
